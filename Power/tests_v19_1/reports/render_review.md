# Final report rendering review

Output: SDTwin_Power_Model_Test_Report_CN_v19.1.docx and corresponding PDF.
Final PDF: 34 pages, 16 numbered figures, 10 case record forms, seven chapters.

The packaged DOCX renderer was attempted but could not locate its LibreOffice
executable. Native Word COM then updated the table of contents, repaginated and
exported the PDF. Poppler rendered all final pages to PNG at 100 dpi.

Every page was visually inspected. The initial standalone chapter heading page
and one-row continuation page were removed. Case-form labels were corrected to
execution method, execution date and record location. Figures remain attached to
captions; table headings repeat on continuations. Chinese text, symbols, legends,
axis multipliers, event source times and measured residuals are legible.

After the final label edit, all pages were rendered again. PNG hashes identified
changes only on pages 7, 9, 10, 13, 16, 18, 20, 23, 27 and 29. Each changed page
was inspected again. All other final pages were pixel-identical to the reviewed
pages. No page was accepted solely by text extraction or a contact-sheet view.

The source report template and user design report were not edited. The numerical
evidence audit verifies the frozen inputs, actual run count, every saved direct
subtraction, and the single explicitly documented event-side sample alignment.
